# LoL-League-off-Limits
A Chromium extension that strictly prevents accidental downloads of Riot Games and League of Legends installers.

Are you tired of accidentally installing League on your PC just to get depressed or disappointed in humanity as you immediately lose 5 games in a row while getting yelled at by the jungler?
This application is for you! It prevents any impulse downloads/installs of Riot applications from the browser.
It closes the pages and helps you recover from an addiction more degrading and depressing than gambling. 

1: In a Chromium-based browser, go to the browser's extensions page/settings.
e.g. edge://extensions/

2: Enable developer mode.

3: Drag the RiotBlocker.crx file into the extensions page.

4: A window opens on the right of your screen asking for permissions, which should be accepted to ensure the extension operates correctly.

5: SUCCESS! You now have an automated impulse control system installed to prevent you from accidental relapses!



NOTE: This is made for fun as a kind of parody of the meme of LoL being the most toxic game on earth. 
Even though I think this might actually benefit some users who have an actual severe addiction to the game.
I, myself, have the bad habit of wasting too many hours on this game for months every time I install it and remove it afterwards.
